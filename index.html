ខ្នងព្រះអង្គ Trekking Guide

Official Trip Expedition Agenda

Altitude 1,301m
Duration 3 Days, 2 Nights
DAY 01

Departure & Basecamp Ascent

Meet at dawn in Phnom Penh. Scenic drive to the basecamp. Initiate the initial 5km mountain ridge climb to reach campsite boundaries before sundown.

DAY 02

Peak Discovery & Stargazing

Sunrise photoshoot on the plateau grasslands. Trek towards Knong Preah Ang summit stone monument. Evening setup for stargazing and open-air campfire.

DAY 03

Eco Initiative & Descent

Participate in mountain waste cleaning and local forest conservation talks. Safe descent hike back to the base transport, returning back home.

⚠️ Essential Gear Recommendations
  • Proper trekking shoes with strong friction support.
  • Windbreaker and thermal layers (temperatures drop significantly).
  • Fully charged portable power banks for continuous communication.
